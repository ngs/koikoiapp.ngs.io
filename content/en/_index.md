## Nintendo rules, faithfully

Yaku, scoring, dealer rotation and draws — the classic Koi-Koi rules as you know them. Yaku progress and "reach" (one card away) indicators make the game easy to learn.

## Features

- **Four AI levels**: topped by a determinized ISMCTS search engine
- **Three ways to play**: tap, drag & drop, or keyboard (arrow keys + Enter)
- **One match = one file**: save games, resume and review anytime
- **Game Center**: leaderboards for wins and best score
- **Multiplatform**: iPhone, iPad, Mac and Apple Vision Pro
