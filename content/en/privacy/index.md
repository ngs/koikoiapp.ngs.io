## Introduction

LittleApps Inc. ("we") is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains how Koikoi - Japanese Card Game (the "App") handles information.

## Information we collect

**The App does not collect, transmit or store any personal information.**

- Game records are stored only on your device (and in locations you choose)
- The App contains no analytics, advertising or tracking
- Everything, including the AI opponent, runs on-device

## Game Center

Leaderboards use Apple's Game Center. Data handled by Game Center is governed by [Apple's Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/). We do not collect any Game Center data.
